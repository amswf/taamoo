Privacy Policy — Tendril

Last updated: September 29, 2026 · Tiny Garden Studio

Short version: Tendril needs no account and collects no personal data about you. It does record anonymous gameplay statistics (for example run distances and which features get used) through Firebase Analytics, so we can make the game better. These records contain no name, no email, no contacts and cannot identify you.

1. What we collect

No personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising SDKs and no third-party trackers beyond the analytics described below.

Tendril uses Google Firebase Analytics to record anonymous, aggregated usage statistics:

  • an random app-instance identifier (not your name, email or device serial number)
  • gameplay events, such as run distance, feature and settings usage
  • device model, OS version, language and approximate (country/city-level) region

This data is used for one purpose: understanding how the game is played so we can improve it. It is processed by Google (Firebase) on our behalf, is not sold, is not shared with advertisers, and is not linked to your identity.

2. What stays on your device

Your game progress (best distance, sun currency, vine skins, daily missions, garden, streak, settings and language choice) is saved in local storage on your device only and is never transmitted. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. Notifications

If you opt in, Tendril may schedule a local daily reminder notification on your device. These notifications are generated on your device and are not sent through any server. You can disable them at any time in your system settings.

4. Sharing features

TENDRIL offers to compose a result card for you to share (e.g. via messaging apps). Sharing happens only when you tap the share button and choose a destination; the game itself sends nothing and stores nothing.

5. Children

Tendril is not directed at children under 13. It contains no chat, no user-generated content and no external links inside gameplay.

6. Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Material changes will be highlighted at the top.
