Tendril: how do I steer the vine?

Touch and hold the left or right half of the screen — the vine bends toward your thumb. Release and it straightens up on its own.

What is the Daily Garden?

Every day all players get the same seeded garden with 3 attempts. Your best distance earns a star rating you can share as a card. It resets at midnight UTC.

How do I earn ☀ sunlight?

Catch sun orbs, graze past thorns, finish daily missions and keep your daily streak alive — the longer the streak, the bigger the multiplier.

I deleted the app — is my garden gone?

Yes, progress is stored locally on your device only. We can't restore it, but every vine you grow is a fresh start 🌱
