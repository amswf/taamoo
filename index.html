ABOUT

A tiny studio with slow hands

Tiny Garden is an independent one-person studio. We make small, gentle games that respect your time and your attention: no accounts, no dark patterns, no timers yelling at you. Just soft systems, hand-tuned numbers and art drawn with patience.

Everything we ship is playable offline first, in as many languages as we can manage, and safe for kids unless we say otherwise.
