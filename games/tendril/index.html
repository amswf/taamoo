Tendril: a vine growing arcade game

Tendril is a one-finger vine growing arcade game for iOS. Steer around thorns, collect sunlight and charge a bloom shield by grazing obstacles. Daily Garden gives everyone the same seeded run, with three attempts each day.

How to play Tendril with one finger

Touch and hold the left or right half of the screen to bend the vine. Release to let it straighten. The vine keeps growing, so choose a route through the thorns before they reach you.

02

Graze thorns to charge the bloom shield

Passing close to a thorn charges the flower meter. Catching sun orbs also helps. Once the flower blooms, the vine becomes invincible briefly and can break through thorns for bonus distance.

03

Three attempts in the Daily Garden

Daily Garden uses the same seed for all players and resets at midnight UTC. You have three attempts; your best distance determines the star rating on your shareable result card.

04

Unlock cosmetic skins through play

Tendril includes more than 10 vine skins and supports nine languages. Skins change the appearance of vines, leaves and flowers without changing gameplay. Sunlight and progress are stored locally on your device.

Frequently asked questions

How do I control the vine in Tendril on iPhone?

Hold the left or right half of the screen to steer. Release to let the vine straighten.

What is the Daily Garden in Tendril?

It is a shared seeded run with three attempts per day. It resets at midnight UTC, and your best distance earns a shareable star rating.

Do Tendril vine skins change gameplay?

No. Skins are cosmetic and unlock through play. They change the vine, leaves and flowers without changing gameplay.

Can I restore Tendril progress after deleting the app?

Progress is stored locally. Deleting the app erases it, and the studio cannot restore that progress.

Can I play Tendril on Android or in my browser?

The released app linked here is on the App Store. The browser prototypes are separate games, not a web version of Tendril.
