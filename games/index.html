RIPPLE PUZZLE

Firefly Pond

Tap the pond to send ripples through the water and guide fireflies into crystal lamps.

How to play: Tap the water to create a ripple. Position each tap so the moving fireflies reach the lamps. Try using fewer ripples on a replay.

40 levels · Chinese interface · Browser prototype

LIGHT PUZZLE

Lantern

Move lanterns through the dark to create a path for a creature that follows the light.

How to play: Drag a lantern to light the route ahead. Guide the creature toward the exit and collect the three pieces of stardust along the way.

40 levels · Chinese interface · Browser prototype

Play Lantern

Try another mechanic: Echo Twins.

TIME REPLAY PUZZLE

Echo Twins

Record your movement, then cooperate with a replay of your earlier actions to complete each puzzle.

How to play: Record a route in the first act, then coordinate with your earlier self in the next act. Collect the energy cores and work toward the exit together.

36 levels · Chinese interface · Browser prototype

Play Echo Twins

Try another mechanic: Firefly Pond.

ONE TAP ARCADE

Starhop

Tap to leave one planet’s orbit and launch toward the next planet in an endless gravity hopping game.

How to play: Watch your orbit, then tap to release toward the next planet’s glow ring. Do not wait too long: orbits decay. Chain perfect releases to build your combo.

One tap controls · English interface · Browser prototype

Play Starhop

Try another mechanic: Lunar Golf.

WORD MINI GOLF

AlphaPutt

Putt through letter tiles in order to spell a word, then sink the ball in the cup.

How to play: Drag and release to putt. Hit the glowing letter next, complete the word to unlock the cup, and finish with as few shots as possible. Avoid the water.

Daily 9 mode · English interface · Browser prototype

Play AlphaPutt

Try another mechanic: Lunar Golf.

GRAVITY MINI GOLF

Lunar Golf

Play mini golf on moons and planets where local gravity changes the arc of each shot.

How to play: Drag and release like a slingshot. Use the dotted trajectory to plan under the current gravity, then land gently in the flag cup. Fewer shots earn more stars.

Drag and release controls · English interface · Browser prototype

Play Lunar Golf

Try another mechanic: AlphaPutt.

DARK MAZE

Sonar

Navigate a dark maze using sonar pings that reveal walls and attract enemies called Listeners.

How to play: Tap to ping and reveal nearby walls. Swipe to move one cell, or hold to keep moving. Remember the route as the light fades and reach the green exit pulse.

Swipe and ping controls · English interface · Browser prototype

Play Sonar

Try another mechanic: Echo Twins.

PHYSICS ARCADE

Yoyo

Fling a yoyo on its string to pop glowing bulbs and build chain combos.

How to play: Drag and release to fling. Aim through several bulbs in a single swing to multiply your score. A fling that hits nothing costs a heart.

Drag and release controls · English interface · Browser prototype

Play Yoyo

Try another mechanic: Starhop.

CHAIN REACTION PUZZLE

Domino

Build a chain reaction with ramps, fans and dominoes to send a ball from a pipe to a bell.

How to play: Drag tools from the tray onto the board. Tap a placed ramp to rotate it, then press PLAY to test the chain. A domino releases a new ball when hit.

Drag and rotate tools · English interface · Browser prototype

Frequently asked questions

Which Taamoo games can I play in a browser without downloading an app?

All nine Greenhouse prototypes listed above run in your browser. Tendril and Panda Go are separate iOS apps available through the App Store.

Are all the browser games in English?

Starhop, AlphaPutt, Lunar Golf, Sonar, Yoyo and Domino have English interfaces. Firefly Pond, Lantern and Echo Twins currently have Chinese interfaces, with English controls described on this page.

Do I need JavaScript to play the browser games?

Yes. The prototypes use JavaScript and canvas for gameplay. The game descriptions and controls on this page can be read without JavaScript.

Where can I report a bug in a Taamoo browser game?

Email support@taamoo.com with the game name, your browser and device, and what happened. Include the steps that reproduce the issue if possible.
