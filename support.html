Frequently asked

Tendril: how do I steer the vine?

Touch and hold the left or right half of the screen — the vine bends toward your thumb. Release and it straightens up on its own.

What is the Daily Garden?

Every day all players get the same seeded garden with 3 attempts. Your best distance earns a star rating (🌱 → ⭐ → ⭐⭐ → ⭐⭐⭐) that you can share as a card. It resets at midnight UTC.

How do I earn ☀ sunlight?

Catch sun orbs (+5 m and ☀), graze past thorns, finish daily missions and keep your daily streak alive — the longer the streak, the bigger your ☀ multiplier (up to ×1.7).

What does the bloom shield do?

Fill the flower meter by grazing thorns and catching orbs. When it blooms you become invincible for a few seconds — smash through thorns for bonus meters!

Vine skins — are they just cosmetic?

Yes! Skins change the colors of your vine, leaves and flowers (in-game and in your menu garden). They never affect gameplay. Everything unlocks through play — no purchases needed.

I deleted the app — is my garden gone?

Progress is stored locally on your device only, so deleting the app erases it. We can't restore it — but every vine is a fresh start 🌱

How do I reset all my progress?

In-game: open the menu → tap GROW → pause (⏸) → "reset all progress". Careful — this erases coins, skins, missions, streak and your garden.

Will there be an Android version?

Planned! We're focusing on making iOS great first.
