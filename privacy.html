Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026 · Applies to Taamoo Studio apps (including “Tendril”), version 1.0.0
The short version: Taamoo Studio apps work entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics SDKs in the Apps. This website separately measures anonymous, aggregated visit statistics — see section 9.

1. Data stored on your device only

The Apps store the following information locally on your device using standard device storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it yourself:

  • Game progress: best distance, current run state
  • In-game currency (sunlight) and unlocked cosmetic vine skins
  • Daily mission progress and daily check-in streak
  • Decorative garden recordings of your best runs
  • Settings: sound, haptics, language

Deleting the App removes all of this data permanently.

2. No accounts, no tracking

The Apps have no login system, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs in version 1.0.0. We do not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or any other personal information.

3. Local notifications

With your permission, the Apps may schedule a daily local reminder about refreshed in-game missions. These notifications are generated on your device — no server is involved and no data is sent anywhere. You can disable them anytime in iOS Settings or by denying the permission request.

4. Sharing

If you choose to share a result card, the Apps use the standard iOS share sheet. Content is shared only to the destination you select. Nothing is uploaded by us.

5. Future versions

If a later version introduces optional services (such as advertising or leaderboards), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy labels will be updated accordingly.

6. Children

The Apps are general-audience games and do not knowingly collect any data from any user, including children under the age of 13.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect or process personal data, there is nothing for us to disclose, export, or delete beyond the on-device data described in section 1. You can erase all of it at any time by deleting the App or using the in-app reset option.

8. Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. This website

This website (taamoo.com) uses Google Analytics (GA4) to measure anonymous, aggregated visit statistics: pages viewed, approximate (country-level) region, device model, browser and operating system type. No name, email or account information is collected, and website statistics are never linked to the apps' on-device data. You can prevent this measurement with your browser's tracking protections or Google's Analytics opt-out.

The game pages (taamoo.com/games/) load Google AdSense, which may use cookies to serve and personalize advertising there. The homepage, the playable prototypes and the apps themselves remain ad-free (see section 2). You can review or change ad personalization at any time in Google's Ads Settings.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@taamoo.com
